# CORNER-D AI Brand

Visual direction:
- Background: near-black (#08050D)
- Primary accent: electric purple (#A855F7)
- Secondary highlight: violet (#B86CFF)
- Text: soft white
- Style: cinematic, futuristic, premium

Brand line:
**Your vision. Our AI. Your story.**

Logo concept:
A crowned geometric D with a play triangle, representing CORNER-D as a creator-first AI video studio.

The included Android vector logo is intentionally lightweight and scales cleanly for launcher/splash use.
